Listen.

Paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link, press Play, and the music starts.

If a track doesn’t start by itself, tap play on the player once; phones block autoplay until you touch the page. With the screen off, SoundCloud often keeps playing. YouTube decides for itself and may pause when the phone locks; press play on the lock screen to continue, or use “Keep screen awake” and lower the brightness.

Queue

    How it works

    1. Copy a link from YouTube or SoundCloud (a song, a video or a playlist).
    2. Paste it above and press Play, or press Add to put it in the queue.
    3. The next track in the queue starts when one ends. Your queue is saved on this device.

    Playback uses the official YouTube and SoundCloud players, so the player stays visible and the music comes from them. Short on.soundcloud.com links aren’t supported; open the link first and copy the full soundcloud.com address.
